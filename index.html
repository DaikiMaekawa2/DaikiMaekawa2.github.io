Nyanmeshi JAPAN was founded with a mission to bring the vibrant, authentic atmosphere of a Japanese Matsuri (festival) to the doorsteps of local communities.

As a Japanese-managed firm based in Singapore, our core team acts as a specialized content engine. By sourcing high-quality tenants directly from Japan, vetting authentic regional products, and maintaining rigorous operational standards, we ensure that every event is executed with precision and Omotenashi (hospitality).

Our vision is to be the most reliable bridge between Japanese culinary traditions and event organizers, providing our partners with seamless, high-performance F&B solutions that shoppers love.
